Customer User Program · October 2026

One trusted customer identity

Customer User is Sunrun's single record of who a customer is, how they log in, and which homes and systems they can access, across the customer app, portal.sunrun.com, InfoHub and Salesforce.

Live in production today. Care uses it in InfoHub, the Sunrun app uses it for verification and self-service, and any app or experience can start using Customer User data now.

Real customer scenarios, handled automatically

New Homes

Customer record created at the builder hand-off, so builder contact info never becomes the homeowner's login.

Service transfers

Buyer becomes the current owner and the seller moves to previous owner, automatically.

Owner & contact changes

Multi-system homes

Verified self-service

Identity model in Salesforce

Customer User, Customer User Login and Customer User System: the person, their verified logins, and every home they can access.

Global login uniqueness

One email or phone can belong to only one person.

Single identity writer

customer-user-service owns create, merge, conflict evaluation, audit and sync back to Contact.

Event-driven provisioning

Salesforce change events (Opportunity, homeowner, Service Contract, Solar Decision, Reassignment) keep identity current automatically.

New Get User

Login reads from a near-real-time replica instead of a cache with a slow Salesforce fallback. One cutover covers the app and portal.sunrun.com.
